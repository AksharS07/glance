# Future Features & Ideas

This document tracks potential future features and ideas for the Dynamic Island project.

## General Ideas
- **The "Shelf/Clipboard" concept**: A space to temporarily hold or clip items (like text, images, or files) directly in or around the island for quick access later.
- **Mini-games/Pets**: Interactive pets or small mini-games that sit on top of or interact with the dynamic island, adding a fun, playful element to the UI.
