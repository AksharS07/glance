Dynamic Island V1.6

What's new in 1.6

Shuffle & Repeat Support

Take full control of your queue. The Island now natively supports Apple Music and Spotify's advanced playback modes including Smart Shuffle.

Keyboard Shortcuts

Control your media from any tab without using your mouse. Use Alt+Space to toggle play/pause, and Alt+Left/Right to skip tracks.

Quick Playlists & Teleport

Instantly launch your favorite playlists right from the extension popup, and teleport directly back to your active media tab from the Island.

Smooth Polish

Re-engineered click targets, removed confusing animations, and fixed Apple Music timing bugs for a seamless, perfectly-synced experience.
