Glance V1.7

What's New

The Focus update — a beautiful blocked page, smarter session management, and tighter controls across every platform.
Focus Blocked Page New
Completely redesigned blocked page with a live countdown ring, accent colour matching your music, staggered entrance animations, and subtle Glance branding.
Smart Close Tab New
Closing a blocked tab now returns you to the tab you were on before — not a random new tab.
Bypass Once New
Temporarily bypass a blocked site for the current session without stopping your focus timer. Hidden in strict mode.
Already-Open Tab Redirect Improved
When a focus session starts, tabs already open on blocked sites are immediately redirected — not just future navigations.
Island Collapse Fix Fix
The island no longer collapses during focus phase transitions when your cursor is hovering over it.
Stale Block Rules Fix
Block rules are now cleared on extension startup so sites can't stay blocked after a session ends.
v1.6.1 — The Polish & Power Update
Shuffle & Repeat Controls New
Native shuffle and repeat for Spotify, Apple Music, and YouTube Music with platform-specific icons.
Keyboard Shortcuts New
Global Play/Pause, Next, and Previous hotkeys that work from any tab.
AMOLED Mode & Lyrics Offset New
Pitch-black backgrounds for OLED screens and a ±0.5s manual lyrics sync stepper.
Sync & Stability Improved
Tighter 400ms drift tolerance, stricter MV3 sandboxing for Apple Music, and debounce locks to eliminate desyncs.
